\section*{Contributions by Others to this Submission}

\begin{instructional}
Start this section on a new page. This submission must be the Protocol Policy Lab's original work. Explicitly acknowledge any contribution from co-authors, peer reviewers, interview subjects, data providers, or institutional partners, clearly indicating the nature and extent of their involvement.

A substantive contribution covers at least one of: (i) conception and design of the submission strategy, (ii) acquisition of evidence requiring significant intellectual judgement or planning, (iii) analysis and interpretation of the evidence, (iv) drafting significant parts of the submission or critically reviewing it.

Present each chapter's collaborators in a four-column table. Percentages within each column should sum to 100\% across all contributors for that area. Ensure the Protocol Policy Lab is the lead contributor for every chapter.
\end{instructional}

\begin{instructional}
{\Large Submission Structure and Formatting Requirements}\par\medskip

Follow the formatting required by the receiving committee. Where the committee has not specified a format, default to: 1.5 line spacing for body text, 11pt or 12pt serif (Libertine or Times New Roman), 20mm margins.

\paragraph{Personal information} Do not include personal contact details, residential addresses, or signatures of interview subjects. Submissions tabled by a parliamentary committee become publicly accessible unless an embargo is granted.

\paragraph{Structure} The order below works for most policy submissions: Table of Contents; List of Figures; List of Tables; List of Abbreviations and Notation; main chapters (Introduction, Background, Response to ToR, Evidence and Analysis, Recommendations, Conclusion); Bibliography; Appendices.
\end{instructional}
