\begin{instructional}
    \noindent
    \textbf{\Large Submission Structure and Formatting Requirements}\\

    \noindent
    Follow the formatting required by the receiving committee. Where the committee has not specified a format, default to: 1.5 line spacing for body text, 11pt or 12pt serif (Libertine or Times New Roman) for the body, 20mm margins.\\

    \noindent
    \textbf{Personal information.} Do not include personal contact details, residential addresses, or signatures of interview subjects in the submitted version. Submissions tabled by a parliamentary committee become publicly accessible unless an embargo is granted.\\

    \noindent
    \textbf{Structure.} The order below works for most policy submissions:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Table of Contents
        \item List of Figures
        \item List of Tables
        \item List of Abbreviations and Notation
        \item Main chapters in logical order (Introduction, Background, Response to ToR, Evidence and Analysis, Recommendations, Conclusion)
        \item Bibliography or References
        \item Appendices (data tables, ethics approvals, raw transcripts, code listings)
    \end{itemize}
\end{instructional}
