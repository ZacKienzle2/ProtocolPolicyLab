\chapter{Introduction and approach}
\label{ch:qon:intro}

This document answers a question on notice taken at the hearing of 1 May 2026, on the Norwegian model and the European standards governing data centres and the data supply chain. The inquiry to which it contributes is itself a novel exercise, described by its proponents as a process to \enquote{establish a parliamentary inquiry into data centre developments in NSW, the first of its kind in Australia} \autocite{greensNSWDataCentreInquiry2026}. That novelty matters, because it means New South Wales is forming its evidentiary and regulatory baseline at the same moment that the sector is expanding fastest, and the comparative models set out below show how other jurisdictions are addressing the same problem.

\section{Evidence base and method}
\label{sec:qon:method}

The answer draws on the primary regulatory and policy literature of Norway and the European Union, including the Norwegian Government's data centre strategy, resolutions of the Norwegian Parliament, the European Union's Energy Efficiency Directive instruments, and European Parliamentary Research Service analysis. Quotations throughout are reproduced verbatim from the cited source.
